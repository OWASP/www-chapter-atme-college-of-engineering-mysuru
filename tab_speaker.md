## Call For Speakers

Our Call for Speakers is now open! If you're passionate about cybersecurity and want to share your knowledge with the OWASP ATMECE Student Chapter, we'd love to hear from you. 
To get started, please review and agree to the [OWASP Speaker Agreement](https://www.owasp.org/index.php/Speaker_Agreement), then send us your proposed talk title, abstract, and speaker details — either via [Email](mailto:mohithkrishnak@owasp.org) or by filling out the Application Form below.

[*Become a Speaker*](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScPH2j0N3lm_jrnLXxKqGN850RGwF6LM5MlqGo_f1T0znUFYw/viewform?usp=header).

**Email Format :**

- Speaker name
- Job Role
- Company / Organization
- Country
- Email ID
- Contact Number
- Speaker Profile
- Presentation Details
    - Name / Title of the Presentation
    - Abstract of the presentation
    - Presentation time required

Once your submission is received, our Chapter Leaders will review it and get in touch with you about your speaking opportunity. Thank you for your interest in the **OWASP ATMECE Chapter!**
